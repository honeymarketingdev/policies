Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 9, 2026  ·  Last updated: May 9, 2026

Honey Marketing ("Honey Marketing," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect personal information when you (a) visit honeymarketing.com (the "Site"), (b) authorize one of our applications — including the Honey Mousetrap content publishing application — to access your accounts on third-party platforms such as Pinterest, or (c) otherwise interact with our services. By using the Site or authorizing our applications, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Who we are

Honey Marketing is a marketing services company headquartered at 7900 McCloud Road, Suite 400, Greensboro, NC 27409, United States. We provide content creation, scheduling, publishing, and reporting services to small and mid-sized brands. Some of those services are delivered through proprietary applications we operate, including the Honey Mousetrap content publishing application.

2. Information we collect

We collect information in three buckets.

Information you give us directly. When you contact us, sign up for a service, or authorize one of our applications, you may give us your name, email address, phone number, business name, business website, and similar contact and identifying information.

Information we collect when you authorize an application. When you authorize a Honey Marketing application (such as the Honey Mousetrap) to access an account you control on a third-party platform (for example, your Pinterest Business account), the third-party platform sends us authorization tokens and certain account metadata that you have agreed to share through that platform's permission screen. This may include your platform username and account ID, the boards or channels you have authorized us to read or write, the pins or posts our application has created on your behalf, and engagement metrics for that content. We do not receive your password to any third-party platform; we only receive the scoped tokens you have explicitly granted.

Information we collect automatically when you visit our Site. When you visit honeymarketing.com, we may automatically collect information about your device and how you interact with the Site, including IP address, browser type and version, pages viewed, time spent, referring URLs, and similar information collected through cookies, log files, and similar technologies.

We do not knowingly collect health information, financial account numbers, or other sensitive personal information through our Site or our applications.

3. How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our services and applications, including publishing content to platforms you have authorized us to access on your behalf.
  • Communicate with you about our services, respond to inquiries, and provide customer support.
  • Generate, schedule, and post content for your authorized accounts according to the workflows you and we have agreed on.
  • Produce reporting and analytics on the performance of content we have published on your authorized accounts.
  • Improve our applications and services, including through aggregated and anonymized analysis.
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent, abusive, or unlawful activity.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations and enforce our terms.

4. How we share information

We share personal information only as described in this Policy.

Service providers. We share information with vendors who provide infrastructure, hosting, analytics, email delivery, error monitoring, and similar services on our behalf. These vendors are contractually required to use the information only to provide services to us.

Third-party platforms you have authorized. When you authorize one of our applications to act on your behalf on a third-party platform such as Pinterest, we exchange information with that platform as necessary to perform the actions you have authorized. The third-party platform's own privacy policy governs how the platform uses information.

Legal requirements. We may disclose information if we are legally required to do so, or if we have a good-faith belief that disclosure is necessary to comply with a subpoena, court order, or other legal process; to protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of others; or to investigate fraud or security issues.

Business transfers. If Honey Marketing is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will provide notice before personal information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

We do not sell personal information for monetary value, and we do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

Our Site uses cookies and similar technologies to enable Site functionality, remember preferences, measure usage, and improve the experience over time. You can disable cookies through your browser settings, but doing so may affect Site functionality. Information about managing cookies is available at allaboutcookies.org.

6. Data retention

We keep personal information for as long as is necessary to provide the services you have requested, comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Authorization tokens for third-party platforms are kept only while your authorization is active; if you revoke our application's access through the third-party platform's settings, we will delete or invalidate the corresponding tokens promptly.

7. Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information against loss, theft, misuse, and unauthorized access. No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to access, correct, delete, port, or restrict the processing of your personal information, and the right to object to certain processing. California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, including the right to know what personal information we collect and to request deletion. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information in Section 11. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

9. Children's privacy

Our Site and our applications are not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in jurisdictions where that is the applicable threshold), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us using the information in Section 11 and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. The "Last updated" date at the top of this Policy indicates when the latest revision took effect. We encourage you to review the Policy periodically.
